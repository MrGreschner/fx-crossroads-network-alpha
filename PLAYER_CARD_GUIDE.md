# Your Crossroads player card

Open **Profile / Mastery / Town → Profile** in town. Scroll down to choose a portrait, rename your player, and download/upload your save.

## Portraits and stickers

Unlock a hero’s first mastery technique to make its portrait selectable. Unlock its final alternate to add a gold star in the portrait’s top-left. These rewards depend on earned progression, not which technique you equip. Fully mastered heroes means heroes with the final alternate unlocked.

| Realm | Sticker |
| --- | --- |
| Astral Rift | Riftbreaker |
| Chimevault | Silencebreaker |
| Ebonwake | Dawn Survivor |
| Shattered Oath | Oath Unbroken |

The sticker collection recognises the Expert and Extreme difficulty categories. Historical clears already saved in your profile qualify. A sticker changes appearance only; it adds no combat power.

Lifetime damage retains previously saved damage. Realm clears count successful realm runs from v70 onward, including repeats. Earlier saves combined realms with Defence/Descent milestones, so their original adventure total appears separately and is not guessed into the new realm counter.

## Gladiator records

Regular and Random Hero arenas have independent records. KOs are opposing real players defeated. Enemy totals also include arena monsters. Deaths and both streak types here are exclusively arena records.

A match win extends your undefeated match streak. A draw preserves it. Losing, leaving an active match or reloading during one ends it. Your best streak remains. A separate knockout streak counts opposing players defeated before your next death; it starts fresh each match. Rescue abilities that keep you alive do not count as deaths.

| Best consecutive match wins | Trophy |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Bronze |
| 3 | Silver |
| 5 | Gold |
| 10 | Platinum |
| 20 | Astral |

The trophy keeps your highest earned tier. Team victories use team score. In a free-for-all, the unique top scorer wins; players tied for the highest score draw, and lower-scoring players lose.

The host selects **No items / food** and/or **Random heroes** before Start. No-items disables purchases and item use and suppresses food for this match; packed food remains available afterward. Random assigns a hero for the match, uses that player’s existing mastery progression, and records results in its own trophy track. Guests see the host’s rules but cannot change them. All participants should use the same game version.

## Build together

| Town upgrade | Unlock | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fellowship Pavilion | 1 successful co-op adventure | Meeting place with team preparation tips |
| Relay Workshop | 3 co-op clears across at least 2 different realms/quest locations | Bakery can provide Hearty Stew as the one free provision after a successful adventure; build the bakery too |
| Expedition Observatory | Clear 10 Descent floors with a party | Telescope upgrade and party records board |
| Veteran Gate / fortified wall | Clear Gate Defence wave 30 | Permanent stronger wall artwork; does not grant hidden defence power |

Co-op requirements need a party of at least two. The wall can be earned solo or together. Projects belong to your personal profile, not a shared town on the room server. Build eligible projects in **Town projects**; the wave-30 wall builds itself. Teacher testing unlocks visuals but does not earn normal records.

## Keep your card

Progress saves locally and travels in the downloaded profile. Download before leaving a shared computer; upload that latest file on your next device. There is no online leaderboard or account server. Multiplayer progress saves periodically and at results; a sudden browser crash can lose the most recent few seconds. A reloaded active arena is counted as an abandoned loss so refreshing cannot preserve an unbeaten run.

# Crossroads: Realms Beyond — V198.45

Build **V198.45-market-guidance**. Godot 4.5.1; network protocol 11 unchanged. Curriculum guidance edition academy-6.

## Market freedom and depth

The catalogue is five times V198.44 in each requested category: **560 products/services, 385 materials, 250 equipment choices**. There are 1,195 native SVG illustrations across products, materials and equipment. New product families include deli sandwiches and wraps, pies/tarts/preserves, soups and produce baskets, pasta, breakfasts/snacks, desserts, speciality drinks, garments, woodcraft, mineral goods, seafood and flowers.

Inherited menus are optional recommendations. Goods businesses may mix goods categories; recipe ingredients, equipment, storage, staffing, costs and production still apply. For example apple pie explicitly requires apples, flour, sugar and butter, plus its equipment. Relevant employee domain experience now follows the product being made in mixed shops. Salon, daycare, mini-golf, rental and courier services retain suitable specialist operating models.

The remaining empty premises can start as **Mixed goods**, with no stock or equipment, and add products individually. Specialist service starts remain available. Products/equipment are searchable, category-filtered and paginated at ten entries per page. Up to 40 product plans may be kept per business; removing a product from the menu leaves its existing inventory and history recorded.

Seeded product demand changes on three-workday cycles, with market-event modifiers. The outlook is visible and varies across saves; it is not a guaranteed sales prediction. Actual sales history is shown separately.

## Clearer market interface

- Removed the long top list of business tabs. Select premises on the map or District map.
- First-visit welcome explains each business and the basic operating sequence. Help remains available.
- Reserve is always shown in the title, with a direct **Reserve • transfer** panel. It starts at zero.
- A prominent **WORKDAY** display shows the selected business's current day, remaining active time and completed full days. District completed openings are labelled separately because multiple businesses can trade together.
- **Sales & trends** shows named-product bars, last-day comparisons, daily units/profit and the separate demand outlook.

Full workdays remain three active minutes. Early closure records results but does not earn a completed profitable full-day count. Existing public five-venture progression remains.

## Academy and teacher workflow

All **93 outcome parts in 23 modules** now have explicit actions, tool locations, deliverables and a worked example. Part buttons replace the ambiguous outcome dropdown. The page explains that a module has several outcome parts and one module submission; partial work may still be submitted for feedback. The main outcomes/rubric remain the grading basis, not a checklist of curriculum subpoints. Existing responses and frozen submissions are retained. Active older definitions receive current guidance without changing outcome IDs.

Students receive only currently assigned module definitions. Unassigned/archived modules are hidden and their lessons cannot be opened through the former practice/legacy routes. Withdrawn-access work remains stored; it is not deleted. The public creation Studio remains open independently.

Manage Class Pro → **Learning Academy → Venture access & progress** opens a student picker. The student row has the same direct button. Choose a module context, tick permitted ventures and press **Save venture access**. On wider screens permissions sit beside current saved cash, workday, employees, inventory, loan, reputation, profitability, product sales/misses and a daily sales graph. Refresh is explicit and the last-save time is shown. These are latest saved, self-reported simulation figures; they do not award marks. Frozen submission assessment remains in Submissions.

Venture permissions apply to the student's shared classroom district across modules. They do not buy businesses, grant money or override acquisition costs. School/teacher ownership checks and policy revision protections remain.

## Fishing

Legendary catches now receive gold celebration lettering, a matching border and a short entrance reveal inside the existing keep/release panel. Mystical and Fabled use their own rarity colours. Trophy-size status remains separate; catch odds, records, XP and keep/release rules are unchanged.

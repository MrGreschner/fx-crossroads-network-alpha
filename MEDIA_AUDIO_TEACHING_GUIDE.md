# Crossroads photography and sound design

## Access and capture
Normal students: say media to Elder Ram. No password or unlock powers. Normal account progress remains active. Game Dev receives the same tools but cannot save portfolio progress.

Open Photo & film tools. Enter a title and explain your framing, audience or revision. Select an optional guide and frame shape. Capture PNG gives three seconds to close tools and stage the action; it automatically hides UI in the resulting photo. F9 hides tools for OBS, F10 hides gameplay HUD and F8 captures. A crop changes the exported photograph, not the gameplay camera or combat range.

Download the full PNG to keep high-resolution work. My portfolio stores the last eight small images and captions. Download Profile carries these, creations and progress to another computer. The photography/ folder gives teachers easy access to images and written reflections. Download a separate reflection report from the portfolio if useful.

## Photography mini-assignment
Create an establishing shot, action shot, character portrait, leading-lines image and negative-space image. Choose one to revise after peer feedback. Explain subject placement, audience and the reason for a change. Submit photos and the reflection report. Virtual photography teaches composition/timing/storytelling; students also need physical-camera practice for exposure, focus and lenses.

## Audio mini-assignment
1. Select an ability animation and describe its anticipation, impact and tail.
2. Produce a short original effect in FL Studio. Trim silence and fade edges to prevent clicks. Avoid clipping.
3. Export WAV or MP3: no more than four seconds / 128 KB. A short mono export is usually sufficient. Longer realm music is submitted separately to the teacher.
4. Upload in Ability Lab → Sound. Adjust level/pitch, listen, then replay with the animation. Save to Workshop or Apply attack and save the hero.
5. Compare with an example sound. Explain how rhythm, timbre and pitch communicate the action and how your mix leaves room for other sounds.
6. Download the ability or full profile ZIP. Audio is embedded; no separate file links are needed.

Effects play on cast/release, not on every damage tick. The global Sound button mutes them. Level 3 shows the real cached playback code with objects, loops and bounds.

## Promotional production
Make a 20-second OBS trailer with an establishing shot, gameplay, studio creation and an ending. Plan shot order and audience. Score it in FL Studio; keep music from masking effects or narration. Compare a landscape and portrait edit and explain distribution choices. This connects photography, broadcasting, audio, design and business communication.

## Grade-0 evidence
Record three issues or improvement opportunities: steps to reproduce, expected/actual results, affected mode/device and a proposed fix. Attach diagnostic information for performance issues. Evaluate both usability and the creative result. Save/download before leaving a shared computer.

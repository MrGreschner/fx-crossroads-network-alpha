# v147 — Progression and practice continuation

## Desktop update notice
Desktop builds v147 onward check the Render service's `/release` JSON endpoint once at launch (8-second nonblocking timeout). When the published build is newer, a notice tells players to return to the main menu, **download their profile ZIP first**, then open the itch.io downloads. Starting another run or joining/hosting requires updating once a newer release is known. No automatic installer or download runs. Offline play remains available when the release check cannot reach the service; Options offers a retry.

Render also rejects outdated game host/join requests with an ordinary error explaining the save-first/update steps. Older downloads do not have the new launch checker; their online room errors provide the instructions after deployment. Everyone should install v147 once. Studio/classroom sharing protocols and room protocol version 32 are unchanged.

## Continue • practice (solo defeat)
The defeat window includes Continue for solo official realms, solo Ram Descent and solo Gate Defence when the player is defeated. Full health, a short safe recovery period, the same position/enemies/floor, XP, level-unlocked abilities and run upgrades are retained. Remaining enemies are not reset or healed. This is a live practice continuation, not a persistent mid-run save.

Choosing Continue rolls back mastery damage and other account awards earned by that entire attempt, including before defeat. Subsequent damage/clears cannot award mastery, points, titles, trophies, daily/weekly/crown progress, personal bests, town projects, quests or realm unlocks. Descent best-clear and best-floor save data are restored to their pre-run values. Normal legitimate progress from earlier attempts remains. Mine/market economy timers keep running independently.

**Enemy discoveries still save** to the realm journal and Mistglass collection. The game shows a Practice Continue label. Returning to town or starting Play again removes the temporary restriction; a fresh legitimate run can earn progress. Repeat continuations are possible. Continue is not offered in multiplayer, PvP, the tutorial (which keeps Retry tutorial), Training Yard or the Mistglass custom practice rift. Continued solo sessions cannot accept late joining friends.

## Realm access
- Fresh profile: Whispering Grove only.
- Clear Grove: Quarry, Honeyglass Gardens and Elementary School unlock.
- Clear **two different beginner realms**, counting Grove: all medium realms unlock.
- Clear **two different medium realms**: hard realms unlock.
- Clear **two different hard realms**: expert/extreme realms unlock.

Repeated wins in one realm do not replace distinct clears. Existing legitimate cleared realms remain accessible, and existing profiles derive tier access from their clear history. Portal, Wayfinder, Adventures and the co-op host picker show locked realms/requirements. The co-op host must have access to launch a realm; friends can join that host's unlocked realm and earn their own legitimate clears. Quests, Training, Studio tests and other game modes are not restricted by these tiers. Playtest and GameDev have all realms open.

## Idle Mining depth
Shafts 1–4 require no Descent clear. Opening shafts 5–10 requires clearing Descent floor 5. Shafts 11–20 require clearing floor 10. These are cleared-floor records, not merely entering a floor; co-op Descent clears also count. Existing mine shafts remain in place. All existing level, material, ventilation, drainage and build-time requirements still apply. The UI states the missing Descent requirement.

Official hero attacks/artwork are unchanged. The release adds no compulsory writing or education assignments.

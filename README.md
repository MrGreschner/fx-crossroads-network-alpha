## Controls

WASD moves, mouse aims, left click is primary, right click secondary (level 3), Space special (level 6), Shift dodges (2.5-second cooldown, still vulnerable), E interacts, and 1–3 use consumables. Primary is available at level 1. Passive unlocks at 5. Level 8 adjusts an attack; level 10 selects mastery. Each realm run starts at level 1 and gets 100 town gold before shopping. Kills add a bounty. Best solo clears save in the local browser.

## Modes

- Realm speedruns: solo in the main game; large co-op maps for two to four players through the Co-op room option. Boss spawns after clearing enemy waves; co-op spawns more and stronger enemies.
- Gate Defense: one to four players, three long approaches, waves indefinitely until the gate falls. Personal gold comes from personal kills, guard upgrades cost gold, and Give 20G transfers gold to the next teammate. Guards fight but do not award bounty to the host.
- PvP Arena: two to four players, free-for-all or four-player 2v2, with neutral enemy waves and respawns. Host selects the format. Neutral kills grant XP.
- Ram Descent: solo procedural obstacle layouts and escalating floors with a local best-floor record. Leveling continues beyond 10 in the arcade modes.

The multiplayer scene uses the same room service and WebRTC signaling proven in FX Connection Lab. Web export is required for browser WebRTC. The host browser is authoritative for attacks, damage, enemies, gold, and upgrades. The service only handles room codes, and it does not store profiles. Test a full match with multiple school computers before replacing a public build. Headless Godot checks are included under `tests/`; browser multiplayer should also be checked on the school devices.

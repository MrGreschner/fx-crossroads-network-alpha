# Crossroads V225 — hero proportions and Lyra's storm hood

- Hazel Hex: softened plum/auburn palette and matte shading, slightly smaller head and a 57 px visible gameplay height. Witch hat, broom and clean face retained.
- Hearth Herald: smaller head/ears relative to torso, shaded segmented armadillo shell and lantern; 56 px visible gameplay height.
- Crossroads Cat: lower curled tail, charcoal fur and purple scarf retained; 49 px visible gameplay height. The apparent old 'hat' was the raised tail.
- Crosswind Striker: all world poses reduced 11%, portrait also reduced. Soccer-ball identity, projectile and gameplay unchanged.
- Echo Dancer: smaller folded cloth scarf, clearer arm/sabre silhouette, matte shading. No wing-like shoulder blocks. Visible gameplay height remains 59.8 px.
- Lyra Galeweaver: pointed witch hat removed; asymmetrical partly raised navy storm hood, silver hair, cropped top, short skirt and boots. More confident face and taller body proportions. Visible height is 60 px. Staff stays on the same side in all six poses.
- Alderjaw: all world poses and portrait reduced 9% (48 px visible height).
- Agent Smith and all other hero sizes/art remain unchanged.

Each replacement has six 80x80 transparent poses. Two cached alternate foot/paw poses preserve the exact idle head/torso position and foot baseline; abilities remain front-facing and direction-neutral. All sprite scaling is visual only; collision, reach, damage, balance and records rules are untouched. The V224 fixed camera and opening remain.

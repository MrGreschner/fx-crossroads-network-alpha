# v129 — Build enemies, retry First Steps and stage your own battles

## Enemy Lab
- Four poses before Decisions.
- Six editable decision presets: Attack, Keep distance, Charge & flee, Sentry, Ambusher and Support patrol.
- One to three attack slots with live visual previews. Melee, projectiles, buffs, healing, control and summons remain editable in Ability Lab. Saved custom attacks can be assigned to each slot.
- Older enemy projects remain readable; sharing/export includes the optional third attack.

## Town and tutorial
- Studio moved southwest, left of Squirrel Post and below the future mine. New connecting path; town space extended south.
- Starter cards show dual roles where applicable, including Living Book, Dribble and Crosswind Striker.
- Tutorial defeat shows the killer's name, attack and portrait, with Retry tutorial highlighted first. Town and adventure options remain available.
- Bramble and Bramble Captain use attack poses and visible strike sweeps.
- New skills pause for a one-second celebration, then show the instructions. Buttons stay disabled briefly and wait for mouse release to avoid accidental dismissal.

## Mistglass Rift
- Mistglass retains its enemy field guide and adds a searchable battle builder.
- Pick up to ten enemies across three kinds. Read portraits, base stats, behaviour and attack information before starting.
- Enter a small blue arena with the current hero at level 10, following a countdown. Replay or change the lineup afterward.
- Echoes use base stats and bounded sparring AI, including melee, ranged spacing, bursts, tracking shots, lobs, healing and summons. Realm puzzles and bespoke boss phases are simplified in this practice arena, as explained in the builder.
- These practice battles award no mastery, realm clears or profile rewards. Game Dev opens the entire catalogue without saving discoveries.

## Broadcasting
- Arena retains Overview and adds Action (recent damage) and Tour (cycles living heroes). Use the dropdown or C to cycle cameras.
- Battlegrounds and Ram Descent now have the same separate spectator-host seat and cameras. Descent allows four adventurers plus the spectator; Battlegrounds allows six or eight fighters plus the spectator.
- Spectators are excluded from combat, team counts, difficulty scaling and profile awards.

## Deployment
Use DEPLOY_V129.md. This is the editable Godot project, not a pre-exported Web or Windows build. The official hero roster and authored hero attacks are unchanged.

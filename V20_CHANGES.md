# Version 20

## Astral Rift

Attack bands now use a faint translucent fill and a thin outline so players remain visible. Timed trap fills are also lighter. Eclipse Engine special attacks launch radial star pulses (29 damage), staggered travelling waves (32 damage), or a delayed large burst (35 damage). Travel and windups give players a chance to dodge.

## Ram Descent

Dungeon size is now 2400 × 1700 instead of 6000 × 3600. Six separated rocks provide cover without dense obstacle clusters. Blocked enemies follow grid waypoints rather than repeatedly sidestepping the same rock. Enemy pools and the gradual floor progression are preserved.

## The Chimevault

Three connected, deterministic maze floors: Echo Vestibule, Bell Foundry, Silent Crown. Defeat every enemy and the floor's Gongbreaker to unlock its lift. Floor three also contains the Silence Regent. A lift moves the whole co-op party, including downed players who still need reviving.

Tuning Wraiths fire pulse fans. Resonance Beetles launch delayed explosive mines. Gongbreakers move slowly, have 1100–1700 base HP, and use telegraphed 45–55 damage strikes and pulse trains. Silence Regent has 2400 base HP and alternating radial waves and staggered ground bursts. Co-op enemy HP scales with party size. Guardians use larger sprites. Original pixel assets are included.

Navigation, collision, stage gates, teleports, attack damage and completion remain host-authoritative. Snapshot data lets joining clients display the correct maze floor, waves and warnings. Maze walls are drawn beneath the actors; travelling wave effects remain translucent and bounded in number.

The new realm is immediately before Shattered Oath. Actual difficulty still needs student playtesting. No Render changes required; export a fresh Web ZIP.

# v162 — Daily Missions and new fishing waters

The main-menu daily activity is now **Daily Mission** throughout the main menu, Adventures, player profile, reward picker and Elder Ram guidance. Challenge Lodge keeps its name, combat trials and weekly featured challenges. Elder Ram separates questions about missions from questions about lodge challenges. The daily rotation, quick-play buttons, eight cosmetic rewards, expiry and existing progress are preserved.

Three new fishing pools add fourteen illustrated species:

| Water | Species | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Garnet Spring | 3 new species | Ram’s Descent, floor 38 |
| Watchwater Bank | 3 new species | North Gate Defence, south bank of its existing river, west of the bridge |
| Silver Canal | 8 new species | Moonveil Masquerade, southern canal |

Moonveil becomes the fourth fishing realm alongside Silverfall Reach, Folded Coast and Lantern Marsh. Town, quests and survival-mode fishing remain available; the existing Descent floor-56 spring stays. There are now **46 fish species** in total.

New species have their own editable vector illustrations, names, rarity weights, size ranges, habitat and lore. Moonveil includes a legendary Crescent Regent. Existing fishing timing, keep/release choices, largest-specimen records, aquarium display tanks, profile-card favourites and portable profiles work with the new species. No bite is guaranteed. Combat keeps running and damage/movement interrupts a cast. Spectators cannot fish.

The floor-38 spring uses a deterministic shore scan shared by host and guests. Both new and old buried springs check the surrounding water space to avoid placing the pool through walls. New artwork is included in the embedded source-art catalog for reliable Web and desktop export.

No gameplay balance, Challenge Lodge rewards or existing fish IDs were changed. The v161 private Game Dev analytics feature remains included.

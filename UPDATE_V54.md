# v54 — Matching hero poses and a greener town
Young Ram, Living Book and Crossroads Cat now use primary attack sprites constructed on the original walking sprites' pixel grids, colours and silhouettes. Book Open and Cat Spin are simplified to match too. Their improved attack effects remain; Dribble is unchanged.

Town additions:
- Trees around the boundary and forest edges, leaving entrances clear.
- A lily pond with four animated swimming fish and water ripples.
- A wooden lookout tower, ladder and softly glowing watch light.
- An animated bonfire beside the quest forest, with flames, embers and a gentle glow.
- Three ram guards patrolling from the north gate towards the forest edges and back.
- Two staffed guard stations outside the gladiator arena.
- Flower beds, benches, mushrooms and two occasional fairies flying between trees.

Pond, tree trunks, tower base and bonfire have simple collision. Guard routes avoid these and existing obstacles. Town residents and well from v53 remain included. No gameplay balance or hero attack effects were changed.

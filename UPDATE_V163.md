# v163 — A gentler first adventure

Whispering Grove now has 24 patrol enemies instead of 30. Three spaced Brambles introduce melee combat before the first archer; a Lantern Moth introduces the next ranged threat farther down the trail. Mushroom throwers are reduced from six to three and appear only in the later glades. The arrival remains safe, the route still builds to mixed encounters, and Rootwarden retains its existing health and attacks. Shared scene placements apply to solo and co-op. Hero stats and other realms are unchanged.

Re-export the Web and Windows builds from this project. Set Render FX_GAME_VERSION to 163 to match the new clients; no new dependencies, disk or analytics settings are needed.

# Version 7

Character Hall now has clickable hero rows and a persistent details panel. Click a hero or use its number to select and inspect health, speed, cost, attacks, cooldowns and passive. Q/E changes pages. Escape closes.

Ram Descent begins with four basic Grove melee enemies at reduced health. Rushers enter from floor 2, simple archers from floor 4, more melee types from floor 5, tougher ranged enemies from floor 7, and homing projectile enemies from floor 9. The complete roster opens at floor 12. Enemy count and health rise gradually. Boss floors occur every five floors.

The town’s Gate Defence entrance is now at the north edge, with a palisade on both sides and ram soldiers flanking the entrance. Training Yard remains in southeast town.

The Medic’s Healing Beacon now restores 40 HP to the Medic and nearby allies in multiplayer. Its previous special incorrectly dealt a tiny damage pulse. Field Dressing remains its 25 HP healing ability.

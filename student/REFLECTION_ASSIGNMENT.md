# FX Crossroads: Design and learning reflection

Complete after the playtest report. Aim for 400–600 words, or use the headings below for a structured response. Your teacher may adapt the format or accept an equivalent oral/video reflection.

1. **Evidence:** Describe one specific thing you observed and how you tested it. What did you initially think, and what did repeated testing show?
2. **Design:** Explain one trade-off. Examples: damage versus reach; speed versus defence; richer graphics versus performance; more options versus clearer menus. Relate it to a real example from your tests.
3. **Players:** How might your experience differ for a new player, a mobile player, a player on a slower school computer or a teammate? Distinguish observed results from assumptions.
4. **Collaboration:** What did another player notice that you missed? If you tested alone, what comparison or second opinion would you seek?
5. **Next step:** Choose one improvement you would develop. Explain how you would prototype it and what result would show it helped. What evidence would change your mind?

Do not retell your entire play session. Connect claims to evidence and explain your reasoning. You may disagree with a design choice while recognising its purpose. Name an uncertainty rather than inventing evidence.

## Suggested assessment — 20 marks

| Criterion | Marks |
| --- | ---: |
| Specific evidence from your testing | 5 |
| Thoughtful analysis of trade-offs and player needs | 5 |
| Reflection on learning/collaboration and uncertainty | 5 |
| Feasible next step with a way to evaluate it | 5 |

Game progress, scores and enjoyment are not assessment criteria. Thoughtful criticism is welcome; explain it constructively.

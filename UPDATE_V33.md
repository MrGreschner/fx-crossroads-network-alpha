# Version 33 — Moonveil Masquerade

Backbeat gains Drums of War: visible, non-stacking 200-unit aura at level five, giving the drum and living allies 8% more damage. PvP opponents are excluded. Healing stays unchanged. Removed the old secondary cooldown passive and updated its generator.

Elara, hero 23, is a human prism mage with original sprite poses. Prism Lance splits once in flight into two weaker diverging bolts; Glass Halo provides close-range orbiting shards; Moonlit Procession creates advancing slowing bursts. Patient Refraction extends her halo at level five.

Her home realm, Moonveil Masquerade, is medium difficulty: moonlit tiled courts, silver canals, glass conservatories, mirrors, gardens, arches and festival lamps. Includes 100 decorative props, 16 solid mirrors, 36 duelists/acolytes and a 1200-HP Mirror Marquis (scaled with cooperative party size). Acolytes fire a new split-lance fan. The Marquis circles the player and cycles between a telegraphed dash, splitting projectile fan and lobbed area blast. Broad main routes and side courts remain traversable. Original pixel assets, editable scene and rerunnable generator included. Realm sits after Silverfall Reach.

Checks pass for aura damage/range/team rules/non-stacking, 23-hero registry and realm scenery, lance splitting and pre-split collision, solo mobile boss/telegraphed dash/projectile fan, cooperative custom boss and health, plus prior v32 mechanics. Physical browser/mobile testing remains after export. Includes all earlier content. Export v33 for itch.io; no Render changes required.

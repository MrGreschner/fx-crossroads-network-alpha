# v143 — Academy, town plan and realm encounters

## Town and education
- Crossroads Studio is a separate public building in the west campus. Its doorway opens the Studio lab hub.
- Crossroads Academy is a larger stone school south of the Studio. Its doorway opens the classroom, education guide and assignment board. It stays locked without unlocked Student or Teacher access; Game Dev may enter.
- Turning off the Student interface does not erase education access or lock a previously unlocked Academy.
- Town streets now use one connected road plan with consistent widths, rounded junctions, short doorway approaches and clear garden courts. The western map and ground cache extend far enough to contain the campus. Relay Workshop, construction lot and Food Stand have separate, walkable approaches. Trees are excluded from roads and campus lots.

## Combat feedback
Positive healing appears in green above the healed player, including life on hit. Overheal shows no invented gain. Nearby ticks combine to keep text readable; feedback is bounded and fades. Respawn/full-health startup does not produce a false combat heal popup. These numbers stay out of town.

## Play again together
Solo replay was already available and remains unchanged. Multiplayer hosts now get **Replay with party** on the results page. It retains the room and roster and uses the existing validated five-second countdown. Guests retain Return to party; only the host can restart.

The 80 official hero definitions and hero artwork are unchanged. This update adds enemy content rather than changing existing hero attacks.

# Crossroads V302 — Ocean World

The 28,000 × 20,000 Coastal Voyage now has 96 distinct scenery designs in varied sizes, 12 irregular rock hazard designs, 16 source materials and 8 animated-water effect textures. There are 323 day scenery placements, 16 exclusive Night Island placements and 32 discoverable landmark groups. Repeated placements do not count as new designs. The offshore map is organized into eight environments: Sunlit Shoals, Glassreef Shelf, Kelp Cathedral, Sundered Fleet, Stormglass Reach, Opal Drift, Compass Deeps and Lantern Trench.

Warm shallows, coral shelves, kelp structures, drowned ships, shattered storm formations, opal ruins, leviathan ribs, pressure bells and lantern wreckage provide different regional silhouettes and palettes. Large submerged landmarks are passable scenery, with a visibly underwater tint; opaque rock hazards use their visible silhouettes for collision. Source art uses soft coloured edges and matte shading; normalized runtime sprites have clean alpha edges and limited palettes. The nine original generated sheets, exact prompts, normalized assets and provenance are preserved with the source.

Each day region has four authored landmark groups, with at most four offshore placements per design plus a few harbour/island anchors. Night Island has sixteen exclusive designs. The HUD names the current region and shows landmarks charted this voyage. Charting is cosmetic, proximity-based and session-only: no new currency, XP, database fields or persistent collection. The chart only labels discovered landmarks. Night Island's position is still hidden until found that night.

Shore Refuge, Kelp Rest, Night Island and the mainland have irregular outlines. The same geometry drives terrain rendering, chart/shore queries, boat obstruction, landing and on-foot boundaries. Night Island's crater has an irregular shared shoreline, retaining the dry bank at (-240,-20) and water at (-120,-20). All existing island centres and nominal radii are retained. The existing coast scene contains no enemy markers; its empty enemy set is unchanged. Oldwater Lake retains all nine original camp enemies and its exact V301 assets/geometry/rendering scripts.

The existing fixed candidate rock positions and exclusion rules remain. Twelve alpha-derived concave rock polygons replace circular ocean collision, with swept movement and the original 25-unit boat clearance. Broadphase bounds include the longest visible tips. Rock impact remains 12 hull, debris 8, immunity 1.2 seconds. No new collision is attached to decorative ruins or buildings.

Water is paged into 140 low-resolution offshore textures plus 20 narrow mainland overlays, with local higher-resolution island textures. Only visible terrain/scenery/hazards are drawn; terrain page cache is capped at 12. Water and scenery size are varied; the original boat and moving debris remain. Wave artwork follows each real moving crest, with original warning lead, force, lifetime and maximum eight-wave pool. Wind ripples, wash, cross-current marks and rotating spiral textures add surface motion.

The existing eddy at (10600,3400) now has visible spiral arms. Two additional remote eddies at (19100,11900) and (11600,16500) use gentle tangential currents, without inward trapping or extra contact damage. Current strength remains capped at 260; existing storm scaling and anchoring rules remain. Fishing habitats/species, bait, gear, cage rules, cargo, releases, capacity, safe-return XP/record banking, session-only voyage rules, storage, combat, repair amounts, boat controls/stats, civil clock, weather and night darkness are preserved. Night Island still moves each real night, stays stable across midnight and returns the player to safe water at dawn.

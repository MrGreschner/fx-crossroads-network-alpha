# Academy heroes — version 8

All version 7 changes are included. Both academy heroes are free and appear on page 2 of the Character Hall (Q/E changes pages). They have their own pixel-art idle, walk and attack poses. Hero details explain their abilities.

| Hero | Health / speed | Primary | Secondary (Lv3) | Special (Lv6) | Passive (Lv5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FX Baseball Player | 110 / 240 | Pitch: 22 damage, 400 reach, 0.65s cooldown | Bat Sweep: 42 damage, 105 reach, 95 degree arc, 2.8s cooldown | Triple Play: three non-piercing balls in a spread, 22 damage each, 440 reach, 10s cooldown | Bat Sweep cooldown reduced 20% |
| FX Golfer | 100 / 225 | Fairway Drive: 30 damage, 560 reach, 1s cooldown | Chip Shot: aimed area strike, 24 damage, 80 blast radius, 330 cast reach, 4s cooldown | Course Ricochet: up to three targets, 34 initial damage with 20% reduction per bounce, 440 cast reach, 180 jump reach, 12s cooldown | Heal 4 HP on each enemy defeated |

Baseball favours medium-range throwing with a close-range backup. Golf favours slower long-range precision with limited area and chain attacks. Neither has piercing primary shots, extra starting health, or unusually fast movement. Specials unlock at the same levels as other heroes. These are initial balance settings; classroom playtesting can reveal adjustments.

Projectile spread and chain specials work in realms, the training yard, PvP, Gate Defence, Co-op and Ram Descent. All versions of those modes use the same hero definitions.

# v94 — Riverfront and party usability

- Extended the north fence to the riverbank and added its stone end pier. The north gate remains open and accessible.
- Removed river-adjacent tree placements whose canopies covered the water. Both banks retain reeds, small stones and the animated river.
- The bridge now leads east along a clear woodland trail to the quest entrance at (2940,1160). A separate, narrower branch leads north to the Chimehouse. Added a fork plaza, trail markers, stepping-stone detail, lanterns, benches and spaced forest framing. Guard patrols follow the eastern trail across the bridge.
- You can click/tap a team, drag your hero, or use Left/Right before connecting to a room. The preview visibly moves sides and its preference carries into hosting or joining. Connected hosts retain authority and team-capacity checks. You cannot start an unhosted or unbalanced match.
- Co-op hero and realm dropdowns are now side by side with a clear instruction line and no overlap.

All v93 main-menu improvements and v92 Battlegrounds are retained. Room service remains protocol 26. No additional Render changes are needed if you already installed service 26. Otherwise follow DEPLOY_V94.md. Export a fresh playable Web build from this source project for itch.io.

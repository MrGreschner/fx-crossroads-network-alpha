# Version 22

Includes all version 21 content. Quarantine Medic’s Boot Kick now adds poison: 1 damage each second for 4 seconds (4 extra damage). Repeated kicks refresh a single poison effect without stacking or resetting its tick timer. Purple indicators show poisoned targets. Works against enemies in solo/co-op and rival players in PvP; allies and invulnerable PvP targets are excluded. The character preview explains the effect. Healing abilities are unchanged.

# Crossroads Market — teacher and student guide

## First session

1. Open Deepwell Mine, commission the camp, and wait or adventure while diggers work.
2. Collect the depot. Ore and gems are materials; Mine Gold funds mining upgrades.
3. Enter Crossroads Market southwest of the pond (or choose Profile → Visit the Crossroads Market).
4. Prepare five Sorted Ore units. This consumes 50 ore and 10 market cash at base quality.
5. Select Sorted Ore, a 12-cash price, Builders, Street Posters and Honest Value. Begin with zero advertising.
6. Write a forecast. Choose a day duration (5 minutes–24 hours). Click **Open Market**, wait for the countdown to close, then read the report and compare sales against profit and cash. You can adventure or close the game while it runs.
7. Repeat with one changed decision. Record why you expect a different result.
8. Download your business report and your player ZIP before leaving a shared computer.

The idle mine uses elapsed real time; the market advances only when a player opens a business day. The market does not require a server. A locally saved personal profile is not a verified online score or competition ledger.

## Three customer groups

| Customer | Natural product fit | Preferred channel | Positioning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Builders | Ore and forged blanks | Street posters | Honest value |
| Collectors | Crystals and pendants | Town gazette | Handcrafted wonder |
| Guilds | Lanterns and clockwork | Guild bulletin | Dependable supply |

Better finishing raises price tolerance and processing cost. Service raises conversion/satisfaction and wages. A larger shop increases traffic and rent. Advertising increases reach most when the channel matches the audience. Higher prices reduce conversion, while very low prices can consume all stock with a poor margin.

## Interpreting financial results

- **Revenue:** sold units × selling price.
- **Cash:** balance available to fund preparation, campaigns and upgrades. Stock processing is paid when prepared.
- **Cost of goods sold:** average cost of the units actually sold. Includes processing cash plus mined-material opportunity values (0.45/ore and 8/gem).
- **Operating profit:** revenue − COGS − advertising − daily overhead.
- **ROAS:** revenue ÷ advertising spend, not profit ÷ spend; reports say no paid ads if spend was zero.
- **Unsold stock:** remains in inventory, with its accumulated cost basis. It is not expensed again as cash when sold.
- **Capital investment:** improvements reduce cash but are not counted as a day's operating expense.
- **Loan:** receive 80 cash, owe 100; the 20-cash financing cost is outside reported operating profit. One outstanding loan prevents unlimited recovery grants.
- **Mine reinvestment:** transfers market cash to Mine Gold. It is not new revenue, profit or combat currency.

## Suggested grade-0 learning task

Run six business days. Record your forecast for each. Find three improvements to the simulation, explain how you tested them and suggest a fix. Include your business JSON report and a short reflection.

For a marketing comparison, change one variable at a time. A five-day calendar cycles ordinary traffic, bridge repairs, collector visits, guild procurement and rain. Comparing the same calendar position gives a fairer test than comparing different events. Price alone does not explain all results: stockouts, audience fit, quality and reputation matter too.

## Assessment indicators

| Evidence | What it can show |
|---|---|
| Ore/gem levels and depth | Supply-side progression and mine management |
| Units sold and revenue | Market activity and sales scale |
| Operating profit | Whether pricing covers costs and marketing spend |
| Cash and outstanding debt | Liquidity and working-capital choices |
| Forecast versus result | Reasoning and evaluation |
| Changed variable plus reflection | Experiment design and learning |
| Brand/headline | Student communication/design, assessed by teacher |

Do not grade sales alone; a high-selling business can lose money. Game Dev reports should be labelled as experiments, not earned account performance. Playtest is read-only for the mine/market.

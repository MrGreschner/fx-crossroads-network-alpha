# v56 — Cloudloom, Cairnheart and Dustdevil
Three free heroes bring the roster to 37. No new realm is added.

## Cloudloom
A weather-weaver with a delayed three-raindrop primary (8 damage each), a four-second Tailwind Seam that speeds allies by 30% and damages enemies for 7 each second, and a travelling procession of three storm clouds. Each cloud strikes each enemy once for 28 damage. Gentle Draft adds 2 HP-per-second ally healing to Tailwind Seam at passive level.

## Cairnheart
A badger stonecarver raises three sequential faults, dealing 24 damage once per foe per cast. Stone Hoist raises a cairn beneath a selected enemy, briefly stunning it, then collapses after 1.2 seconds for 36 damage within 85 units. Boss stun is reduced to 0.4 seconds. Rolling Rampart is a travelling 240-unit-wide wall of stone that hits once for 62 damage and pushes enemies back. Bedrock grants 5% temporary damage reduction per primary victim, capped at 20% per cast.

## Dustdevil
An enchanted brass vacuum. Hold primary for Intake: 12 close-range damage each 0.7 seconds, with a one-second slow. The first 0.4 seconds of a new intake can catch one hostile projectile passing the nozzle. Once caught, it is held instead of continuing intake damage. Releasing primary fires a straight returned shot in the current aim direction for 36–60 damage; Deep Filter adds 8 at passive level. Stored shots release automatically after six seconds or stale input. Friendly shots, beams and ground hazards are not captured. Basic shots, shared projectile attacks and lobbed projectiles are supported.
Brush Crush deals 38 damage across a close 110-degree arc. Grand Draw pulls enemies in a visible 320-unit cone towards the nozzle for 2.5 seconds, lining them up for melee; bosses are pulled less. Walls block capture and pulling.

Keyboard/mouse and mobile aim-stick press/release use the same mechanics. Primary hold and release aim are relayed to the host; only the host catches and returns shots. All new effects are included in snapshots and finite/capped. New sprites share their walking/attack pixel grid.

Town realm flags are reduced to five and moved to a clear southern garden display, away from the Training Yard and Journey board. All v55 features remain included. Student guides and ability catalog describe the new mechanics.

Export a fresh Web build for itch.io. No Render service changes.

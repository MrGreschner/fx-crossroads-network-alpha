# v91 — River continuity and distinct hero kits

- The east river now enters at the northern town edge and flows out of the southern edge. It no longer starts in the middle of the grass. The wooden quest bridge remains usable. Chimehouse moves completely onto the east bank, with a path from the forest side; watermill and town upgrade approaches remain connected.
- Merrymask's primary is **String Scissors**. Two puppet strings cross at an aimed point and snap after 0.18 seconds for 24 base damage in a 42-radius area. It has distinct crossing-string graphics and a 1-second cooldown. Broad Stitches mastery covers a larger 65-radius area for 18 damage and a 1.25-second cooldown. Shared Applause repairs nearby puppets when the scissors hit.
- Dust Marshal's primary is **Six-Shooter**, a visible fast pistol round: 22 damage, 430 reach, 0.85-second cooldown. It stops at the first enemy or wall. Long Barrel mastery trades recovery for 28 damage and 560 reach. True Grit still heals 3 HP once when shooting a tied enemy.
- Cindervane's passive is **Ash Rebirth**. From level 5, defeat starts a visible 3-second ash countdown, then revives at the death position with 50% maximum health and brief protection. It cannot move or attack in ash form. The ability can trigger once per 120 seconds of active combat time. Its cooldown survives floor/region combat resets; Descent waits for pending rebirth before replacing a cleared floor. Arena defeats still count as a death/KO. Last Stand or another rescue that prevents actual defeat takes priority. Feathers still power Wing Cuts/Cinder Ascent but no longer grant the former damage-reduction passive.
- Dune Cook becomes **Crumb Cook**, a living layer cake with chef hat, icing, cherries and a spatula. Its four required sprite poses are redrawn. Spatula Chop, Custard Pot and Dessert Banquet retain the support kit's balance; the internal dunecook id and stable hero number 71 are preserved.
- Character Hall ability cards are stacked in this order: Primary (Left mouse), Secondary (Right mouse), Passive, Special (Space), then mastery. Both the hero list and details remain scrollable.

The roster remains 72 heroes. Updated student ability and mastery catalogues are included. Multiplayer now requires room service 25; export fresh Web and Windows builds. Nothing is deployed automatically.

# Version 27 — Damage and ability healing results

Results headings now align with their values, keeping damage dealt clearly separate from ability healing in solo and multiplayer. Damage remains recorded across floors. Ability healing counts only actual health restored by character abilities; potions and automatic end-of-floor recovery do not contribute.

Includes version 26 ranged enemy retreat, firing distance, separation and rock avoidance, and all earlier changes. Checks cover damage accumulation, ability healing, recovery exclusion, overhealing and final results totals. Export a fresh Web build for itch.io; no Render update is required.

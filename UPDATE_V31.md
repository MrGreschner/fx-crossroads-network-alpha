# Version 31 — Broken-world scenery and ranged positioning

The Shattered Oath nexus now has collapsed arches, cracked memorials, torn banners, root tangles, craters and rubble. Fragmented paved spokes lead from the hub toward the portal pads. The final arena has perimeter ruins while its fight space and green exit remain accessible. Includes six original prop textures, broken road texture and a rerunnable scene generator. Adds 79 decorative props and four solid rubble piles at the nexus corners. Four inner trial scenes, enemy counts, traps and guardian behavior remain unchanged.

Shared ranged positioning now covers solo realms, cooperative realms, Arena, Gate Defence and Ram Descent. Ranged, burst-ranged, dual-ranged, homing, summoner, lob and beam/portal casters plus Folded Coast attackers keep distance and separate from nearby enemies. Ordinary realms aim for 245–285 units of space. Ram Descent retains its existing 230–266-unit spacing and grid navigation. Gate Defence still advances toward the gate when unopposed and retains close guard engagements. Chimevault tuning wraiths keep distance when they have a clear route, retaining maze pathfinding otherwise. Bosses preserve their distinctive patterns. Collision and terrain checks constrain retreat choices. Attack damage and cooldowns are unchanged.

Checks pass for thirteen ranged archetype classifications and retreat directions, integrated solo shooter movement, ruins and texture loading, clear portal/final-rift access, the complete solo/co-op Oath progression and exit, Gate Defence movement, and Ram Descent positioning. Physical browser testing remains for the exported build. Includes Patch and all version 30 and earlier changes. Export v31 for itch.io; no Render change is required.

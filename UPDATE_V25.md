# Version 25 — Frostguard balance

Glove Reflect now reduces damage by 50% instead of 90%. A 20-damage melee hit therefore deals 10 damage during the guard, before equipment modifiers. Projectile reflection remains enabled, and duration (1.1 seconds), cooldown (2.2 seconds), and Cold Reprisal passive are unchanged. The ability preview reads the revised reduction. All version 24 content remains.

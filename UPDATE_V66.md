# v66 — Your hero, your town, your journey

Includes every v65 feature and all 43 heroes. Export a fresh Web build and upload that exported ZIP to itch.io. The editable ZIP is not the itch.io upload. No Render changes or account server are required.

## Player profiles

In town, choose **Profile / Mastery / Town**. Give your save a player name. Progress saves on this browser automatically. **Download profile** creates `crossroads-profile.json`; keep it in school storage or on a USB drive. **Upload profile** opens the device's file picker, shows the name/export date, and asks before replacing current progress. Download the old profile first if needed. **Start a different player** also asks before resetting the shared PC.

Profiles include mastery, tuning, titles, town projects, food/crops, journey progress, selected hero, quest saves, realm records and local multiplayer bests. They do not resume an in-progress match. There is no password, automatic cloud sync or shared world server. Progress on two devices is not merged; import the newest download. New progress needs a new download before moving computers.

## Hero mastery

Every hero now has an authored `mastery` section with a unique title and selectable technique. Read the complete list in MASTERY_CATALOG.md. Open the journal from the town button or **Mastery for this hero** in Character Hall.

- Technique: 3 successful adventures, 2 different realms, 5,000 combat damage.
- Master title: 6 successful adventures, 3 different realms, 20,000 combat damage.
- Choose original or mastery technique before an adventure. Original abilities remain available.
- Training does not count. Combat damage from unsuccessful adventures still contributes.
- First success in each different realm grants one point, capped at six earned. Repeating one realm cannot farm points.
- Survival milestones count as adventures: reaching descent floor 3 or defence wave 5 earns the mode's first point; repeats do not grant more points. PvP damage contributes, but PvP does not grant realm-clear points.
- Four points can be equipped, at most two per choice: Power (+4% base attack damage, +5% cooldown per point), Vitality (+5% base maximum HP, -3% movement), Guard (-4% damage taken, -3% base attack damage). Reset or move points freely in town.
- These modest trade-offs apply in solo and multiplayer. Host validates numerical limits and receives each guest's build; snapshots carry the same choices.

Examples: Walking Library allows 30% movement while channeling, at a slower page interval and lower damage. Bracken can equip three timed grenades instead of manual detonation. Tumblebrew can burn instead of poison with Spout Shot. No additional character sprites or overlays are required.

## Town growth

Open **Town projects** for exact requirements and build buttons. Talk to distinct service NPCs, water three marked flowerbeds near the south plaza (E / Interact), donate packed food to Moss, finish quests and clear realms.

Seven projects: Flowerbeds, Pondside Bakery, Covered Market, River Watermill, Hall of Champions, Quest Reading Room and Healer Supply Wing. The river flows and the mill wheel turns. Gardens gain flowers, a bench and butterflies; other projects add their displays and services.

The bakery provides one free provision per successful adventure, never several. The watermill adds an Antidote Pear option and the supply wing adds Cleansing Tea, all sharing the same one-provision allowance. Your food slot must be empty. Free food refunds for zero gold. Market savings are one gold on purchases costing at least ten gold; refunds return the actual price paid. Projects belong to the player's profile, not a shared town server.

## Elder Ram and teacher testing

Elder Ram now offers topics and **Something else…** for typed questions. Answers are prepared locally, not generated by an online AI service.

For teacher testing, type exactly `I am admin, R4m`, then enter `Tx47j!e`. A flash announces testing. All mastery techniques/titles become available, six points are available per hero, all town projects are built, and the local hero starts adventures with its attacks unlocked.

A normal-progress backup is preserved. Choose **Profile → Leave teacher testing and restore my progress** to restore it. Downloads made while testing contain that normal backup. Testing does not earn mastery. This is a local testing shortcut, not a secure administrator account; its phrases are in the exported client code. Change them in `scripts/legacy.gd` if desired.

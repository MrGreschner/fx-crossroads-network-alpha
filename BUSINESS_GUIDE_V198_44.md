# New business guide — V198.44

## Choose your start

The district map has fifteen premises. Public players choose one supported startup; later acquisitions cost reserve savings. You may own five ventures. Sell a closed venture to change your portfolio. Teacher-managed classroom access is independent: teachers choose which premises each student may acquire, and acquisition still costs money.

Each premises retains its own menu, recipes, supplier deliveries, equipment condition, inventory costs, staff wages, pricing, marketing, daily reports and loan commitments. You can rename/rebrand a business through the existing moderated name flow. A blank premises lets you choose a trade and outfit it yourself.

## Services work differently

For salon, daycare and mini-golf, the Products panel lists services and their ingredients, equipment, fee, duration and capacity. Prepare supply kits, then open. A ready kit is not a completed sale. Customers occupy places while work happens and pay only at completion. Extra chairs without stylists, pens without carers or clubs without room will not remove every bottleneck.

The owner contributes to preparation and service capacity. Resume history and assigned roles affect staff effectiveness. Family golf uses two places. Buying suitable equipment and hiring capable staff are separate investments. Closing early cancels unfinished services and records their consumed supplies as a loss.

Prepared service kits use the same supply and shelf-life accounting system as existing products; they represent materials set aside for an appointment, not animals or haircuts being sold from a shelf. The ledger shows their unfinished material value as work in progress.

## Decisions to watch

- Cash is not profit: payroll, materials, borrowing, marketing, upkeep and spoilage still matter.
- Advertising can attract customers that you cannot yet serve. Watch waits and missed requests before spending more.
- Excess ingredients can expire. Match the preparation buffer and orders to demand.
- Equipment wears with use. Repair before reliability and service capacity suffer.
- Compare profitable completed days, profit streaks, losses, customer satisfaction and cash rather than counting openings alone.

These businesses can lose money and fail; starting recipes are a foundation, not guaranteed success. Restart retains the existing confirmation flow. Selling a business also requires confirmation and cannot be used to claim repeated startup support.

## Teacher use

The teacher learning policy lists all fifteen active premises. Unlock selected businesses per student; existing students retain owned locked businesses, but cannot continue trading them while locked. Product plans, recruitment decisions, reports and frozen submitted evidence remain in the teacher review workflow. The course catalogue remains **23 modules and 93 assessed main outcomes**; this release adds venture choices rather than new credits.

Older opportunity plans retain their original chosen business by ID. Existing owned retired empty sites remain accessible in a legacy annex so those saves are not discarded.

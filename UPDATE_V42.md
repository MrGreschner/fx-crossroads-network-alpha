# v42 — Wayfarer Wilds quest expedition

- Moonveil Masquerade moves into the medium group, after Moonburrow and before Space, Zombie, Lantern Marsh and Silverfall.
- Silverfall gains four Tidebinders with original turtle-mage sprites, weaving slowing projectiles and a telegraphed travelling water wave.
- Lantern Marsh gains four Bog Bellringers with original walking-bell sprites and five-shot returning projectile fans. Both new enemy types retain ranged spacing.
- A separate forest quest entrance sits east beyond the Training Yard. The town expands to make room for its path and wooded sign. The regular realm portal list stays separate.
- Wayfarer Wilds is a 10,000 × 11,000 quest world: a broad forest surface, a river with three bridges, Willowrest settlement, Mosswheel Mill, camp and shrine ruins, Glowroot Grotto, and the multi-room Drowned Archive. Textured trails, cottages, farm plots, lanterns, forest props and dungeon engravings add landmarks. A village spring restores 4 HP/second outside nearby combat.
- Three linked NPC quest chains: Edda's guarded waylight glimmers; Orrin's waterwheel and mill reclamation; Sable's three archive seals, Rootbound Keeper battle, and heartbell recovery. The first two unlock the third. New bandits, mushroom slingers and the Keeper complement existing enemies. The Keeper uses a warned charge, lobbed volley, and weaving fan.
- Any hero can enter solo or with a 2–4-player co-op party. Secondary starts unlocked at level 3; turn-ins unlock later abilities. Host-scaled encounters and shared objectives use existing multiplayer authority and snapshots. The quest world does not run the normal realm-wave or automatic realm-boss sequence.
- Quest journal supports scrolling and touch buttons. The map distinguishes objectives, NPCs and dungeon entrances. Target guidance routes through the appropriate entrance when the objective is underground. NPC markers show offer, progress and turn-in states.
- Local quest checkpoints save at acceptance, collection, transitions and turn-in; solo and host co-op campaigns have separate saves. Incomplete combat encounters respawn on re-entry, while collected objectives and completed quests remain. Turn-in rewards are paid once. A fresh solo expedition is available at the town entrance.
- `QUEST_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md` explains how students can append quests, dialogue, NPCs, prerequisites, objective markers and encounters in JSON. `tools/validate_quests.py` checks references and cycles. New campaign quest IDs are added automatically to existing saves. Geographic map expansion and genuinely new objective types still require extending the shared controller.

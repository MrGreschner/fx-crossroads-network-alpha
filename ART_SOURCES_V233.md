# V233 — Studio and Market art

Original built-in ImageGen sprites made for Crossroads: Realms Beyond. Exact prompts are retained in tools/market233_prompts.json, with selected transparent source sheets in assets/market233/*_source.png. Extraction and nearest-pixel normalization are repeatable with tools/prepare_market233.py. No third-party asset packs are embedded.

Nine civilian identities preserve the existing ram, badger, cat, newt, human, fox, hare, owl and proprietor designs. Each has idle, two neutral walking steps and a work gesture; four daycare dog designs also have four poses. The first civilian sheet was revised to remove baked-in bread, vegetables and books from working hands. Real products remain separate simulation-driven drawings. Generated hire profiles retain their species and coat colour through a cached garment-mask variant, without tinting the whole sprite.

The sixteen equipment drawings preserve their real catalogue identities. Other catalogue icons retain their original editable SVG geometry and titles; the renderer adds restrained native-SVG midtone gradients and caches an 80px raster. Equipment placement and all business rules are unchanged. Building roof/floor material details remain code-native and static.

Studio's style explanation uses actual current Young Ram, Crossroads Cat and Living Book frames. New character canvases default to 80 × 80. Legacy artwork is not resized automatically. The in-app clipboard stores an independent PNG and works between the shared pixel editors, including Hero, Enemy, Ability, alternate-form and terrain editors. Hero body slots and Ability Lab effect frames remain separate, with their existing project format and review rules.

## Preservation and rendering
All previous source files are retained. All content/ and scenes/ files are byte-identical to V263, including both realm scenes, routes, portals, collision radii, the four ice momentum rectangles, all enemy/boss definitions, progression and record revisions. No records are reset. The Between/attunement keeps its own expedition map; normal and overcharged Frostbound use the new realm art. V263's town pond bank and Descent 50.5 refuge remain included.

The ground is baked offline at 2 world pixels per image pixel into two culled pages, 1536×950 and 1564×950; neither exceeds 2048 pixels. Props share one 1024×768 atlas and actors one 640×512 atlas. At most two static ground draws are needed, followed by culled props. Maximum decoded new runtime artwork is 16,236,448 bytes (~15.5 MiB); source material images are not loaded at runtime. No dynamic texture generation, lights, shaders, particles or additional physics nodes. Cached textures are shared between views; the old ice ground cache and older polish draw pass are suppressed in the scoped realm.

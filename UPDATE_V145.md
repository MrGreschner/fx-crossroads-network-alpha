# v145 — Civic square, mountain district and Academy workstations

## Town
- Deepwell Mine now sits against the western mountain frontage, nearest Crossroads Market. Ram Descent sits further south along the same range. The public Crossroads Studio occupies the former central mine district.
- Entrances and matching path spurs move with each building. The Academy remains on its own southern campus.
- The Food Stand label now belongs to its actual stall and cook.
- Elder Ram, the portal and wishing well occupy a broad, paved central courtyard with an inset compass, stone seams and perimeter trim.
- The gladiator stadium moves west to leave space before Squirrel Post. It has tiered seats, arches, sand, marble steps and animated flags. Ten recorded PvP wins across standard and random arena matches unlock a larger grand stadium with gold banners and pillars. Bot practice does not grant ranked arena wins.

## Camera and navigation
- Normal profiles: Menu → Options → Screen zoom: 0.75×, 1× or 1.25×. Saved on this device. Aim coordinates use the same inverse transform as the visible world.
- Media/GameDev camera adds 0.5×. Minimize camera controls hides the panel while retaining Media Mode. A compact Camera button restores it; F9 also toggles it. F10 removes gameplay UI for filming, F8 takes a clean photo. Camera restore controls and education controls are excluded from clean captures.
- Top buttons share one layout and height. Student toggle occupies the gap between Menu and Profile; Education Guide is accessed through Menu or Academy, rather than a second oversized top button. Teacher class view and Teacher toggle are in Menu.

## Streamlined education
Routine/safety, basic competency and personal pathway journals are no longer prompted. These outcomes remain available for teacher observation/discussion. Archived entries and frozen submissions remain valid and portable. Project progress ignores the removed prompts.

Teacher class records and outcome review show a transparent suggested mark. It uses prompt coverage, required artifact types and available automatic checks. Complete evidence alone is capped; accuracy, originality and reasoning still require reading. It never writes grades automatically, and observation outcomes receive no automatic score. These are provisional suggestions, not reliable semantic assessment or automatic CTS credits.

At Elder Ram, enter these exact phrases in normal mode:
- `studentModeOff`: permanently removes saved Student access and stops evidence capture, retaining portfolio work.
- `teacherModeOff`: permanently removes saved Teacher access, retaining class records.

The usual unlock codes can grant access again. Exit GameDev/Playtest before changing saved permissions. Download the resulting profile if moving between machines; an older downloaded profile may still contain the older permissions.

## Academy
Six detailed workstations surround an open central aisle: Studio, Assignments, Project ideas, Progress, and two Coming soon stations. The progress station shows work-plus-writing gauges across the supplied modules. In-room gauges refresh on a timer rather than recalculating every drawing frame. Walk through the bottom doorway to return to town; the top navigation remains.

Official hero data, combat attacks, enemy data, save contents and room-service protocol are unchanged.

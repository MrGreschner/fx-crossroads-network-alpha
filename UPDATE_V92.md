# v92 — Battlegrounds

- New 3v3 / 4v4 mode in Gather Your Party, Adventures, and a glowing clearing north of town’s gate.
- Three/four real march paths, one personal barracks per hero, two opposing original factions.
- Eight army roles, persistent per-match recruit counts and personal army upgrades.
- Passive income, kill bounties and XP, central hero item market, seven-second hero respawns.
- Destroy opposing barracks before their team heart becomes vulnerable. Parties stay together for another match.
- Verdant Front woodland map; Iron Rain war-zone map with warned, staggered airstrikes hitting both sides.
- Existing hero combat integrates army targets: melee, projectiles, charges, chains, lobbed blasts, flamethrowers, buffs/debuffs and summons. Support fields can heal allied troops; fallen soldiers support corpse raising and temporary conversion.
- Maximum 24 active troops per owner. Strategic AI uses a fixed tick and spatial neighbour buckets. Drawing culls distant troops and snapshots round movement coordinates.
- Room service **26**, waiting public-room listings include map/team size, exact 6/8 capacities and no mid-match new joins. Existing modes retain their capacities and arena options.
- Town extends north for the entrance; the eastern river continues through that area. Lobby cards and army menus are spaced and scrollable.

All 72 heroes remain unlocked. No new hero or permanent progression gate was added. Read BATTLEGROUNDS_GUIDE.md for rules and DEPLOY_V92.md for installation. This release is editable source, not a playable Web export.

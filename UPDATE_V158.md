# v158 — Clear town signs and market rewards

Healer House and its planned wing now use separate facade plaques, above the door and healer. Vendor canopy planning and supplier labels are separated on the stall front. Wayfinder Lodge's name is on its building rather than across the walkway. Shared building plaques hide when they would cover the controlled hero.

A completed profitable market day with at least one sale earns a 20 CC supply dividend, at most once per real device-calendar day. Unclaimed dividends can accumulate to 60 CC. Collect them on the Market floor tab, after the beginner setup card. Spend the CC on supplies during the current visit/session using the existing game economy. Unclaimed dividends travel in Download Profile; collecting spends that bank entry and cannot be repeated. Ordinary session CC still follows the game's existing reset rules.

There are no direct damage, health or experience bonuses, and multiplayer still starts with its normal CC. GameDev and Playtest cannot earn or collect real dividends. Market business cash remains separate from Crossroads Coins; this capped reward is not a conversion of unlimited market profit.

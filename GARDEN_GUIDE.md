# Seed House growing guide

Plant one unlocked crop, clear an adventure to ripen it, then harvest with an empty food slot. Harvests unlock higher tiers within that family at 1, 3 and 6. Crops and unlocks travel with your profile.

| Iron Plum Tree | orchard | 1 | 8g | Iron Plum |

| Golden Apple Tree | orchard | 6 | 12g | Golden Apple |

# v73 — A town with room to grow

The town expands to 2700 × 1900. The arena moves to its own western plot; the seedhouse, relay workshop, reading room, construction site and fellowship pavilion have separate southern plots. Connected streets, direction boards and clearer entrances organize the civic district. Existing upgrades and saved progression remain supported. Clinic approach trees are cleared.

A two-second FX Crossroads title appears once per launch, then fades gently into the town. Movement is paused during the intro. Returning from adventures does not replay it.

Northeast of the healer house, the new Chimehouse offers a free five-bell timing challenge with buttons for mouse or touch. No currency or combat advantages are awarded.

Willowrest now has a visible woodland trail and Crossroads Town sign at the existing return interaction. Existing solo and party return behavior is retained.

Validation: Godot import, town approach and activity checks, visual previews with all town projects unlocked, and intro lifecycle checks. The older v65 test passed its food/respawn portion but failed obsolete coordinate and multiplayer assumptions; it is not counted as a full regression pass. Actual phones and school PCs still need playtesting.

This is an editable source project. Export Web again for itch.io. BUILD_WINDOWS.cmd produces the Windows package locally; this ZIP does not contain a compiled Windows executable. Render needs no changes.

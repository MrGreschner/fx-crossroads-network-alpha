# Version 34 — Aurel, hero 24

Added Aurel, a small copper-scaled star dragon with original idle, walking and attack pixel art. Starfire Spiral fires alternating curved bolts. Wingbeat is an expanding knockback and slowing gust. Skyfall Constellation forms a glowing overhead star pattern, marks seven landing circles, then launches a staggered shower of visible, glowing meteors and impact blasts. Its 18-second cooldown, 24-damage individual impacts and 65-unit blast radius keep it an area-control special rather than an instant unavoidable screen attack. Afterglow adds 12 units to meteor blast radius at level five.

Added a shared meteor_shower attack style and meteor projectile trails; student ability catalog updated. Solo and multiplayer use the same host-authoritative effects and standard PvP hostility rules. No new realm in this update. All version 33 and earlier content remains.

Backbeat/Moonveil regression checks pass. Browser/mobile playtesting remains for the exported build. Export v34 for itch.io; no Render update required.

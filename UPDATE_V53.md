# v53 — A living town
The PvP entrance is now a miniature gladiator stadium, with stone arches, terraced stands, a sandy fighting floor and crossed-weapon banners. Its entrance works as before.

Five decorative residents (two rams, two humans and a rabbit) walk short routes, pause to water plants, scatter seeds, sweep or deliver a parcel. They are not selectable heroes or combat actors.

The wishing well east of the square costs 1 gold per interaction. Each toss independently has a 1-in-100 chance of a Last Stand, Aegis Capsule or Heart Locket, added to your three-slot town loadout. An empty purse or full pack spends nothing. Coin animation and outcome text show the result. The well uses the existing per-run gold/loadout system, with no purchases or persistent currency. All v52 features remain included.

# v90 — Crossroads grows beyond the bridge

## Town and training

The town extends to 3100 × 2250. A permanent eastern river has one walkable wooden bridge leading to the forest, quest entrance and campfire. The watermill upgrade adds its building beside the river without closing the bridge. A southern fenced garden has an ox farmer. The western mountain contains Ram Descent and a boarded mine marked “Coming later”; the mine intentionally has no interaction yet.

Food Stand occupies the old Descent plaza. Squirrel Post has its own site south of the stadium. Town labels use short fitted backgrounds, the champion statue moves away from Character Hall, and the selected hero stands in the hall doorway. Hall, vendor and stadium have highlighted entrance pads. Two additional paired ram patrols cover the bridge and garden. Existing upgrade buildings retain connected approaches; the observatory interaction has been moved clear of its tower collision.

Speak to the Training Guide at the northern end of the practice yard. Four topics explain attacks, warnings, buffs and progression. Replay First Steps opens the existing first-nine hero selector and short tutorial.

## Confirmable catalogues and discoveries

Choose Realm, Supply Vendor and Food Stand use native scroll containers: drag the scrollbar, scroll the wheel, or scroll by touch. Select a row, then press the large Enter Realm / Buy Item / Buy Food button or Enter. Buying shows the item and amount paid; food remains a separate automatic fourth slot.

Each realm lists unknown enemy/boss portraits as question marks. Encounter a living enemy nearby with an unobstructed path to reveal it for that realm. Discoveries belong to your portable profile, including co-op encounters recorded on each player's device. Meeting an enemy in one realm does not reveal it everywhere. New realm authors should regenerate content/encounter_rosters.json with tools/build_encounter_rosters.py.

## Attack and map changes

Tempo Monk's Bullet Punch has three quick directional jabs for 8 base damage each. Landing all three on one enemy applies a short slow. A miss does not grant the slow. Quill Prowler's claw now has curved talons and falling leaf effects instead of a plain flash.

Honeyglass Gardens has spaced flower/tree props, smaller visible trunk collision footprints, softer trail corners and quieter trail edges. Large flowers fade when the player approaches their crowns. Its maze, defenders and boss progression remain connected.

## Progression and release

First techniques require 10,000 damage; final techniques require 85,000 damage. Other clear/distinct-realm requirements remain. The Foundry pair also needs 10,000 damage in addition to its special co-op duet clear. All 72 heroes remain unlocked at the start.

# v57 — Training targets and two new quest journeys

Training Yard now has five stationary dummies, including a central bounce target, plus a moving target on a cart. Two launchers fire real, visible projectiles with staggered warning flashes. Practice blocking, catching, dodging and destroying shots. Practice damage is nonlethal and health regenerates; targets grant no XP.

Veyra Spellthief follows Snowdrift. Pippa Fernwhisker follows Polarity. The roster remains 37 heroes.

Wayfarer Wilds expands east into two connected regions, bringing its campaign to five quests:

- **The Last Summit Bell** — complete Waylights, then speak to Tamsin in Willowrest. Activate trail beacons, climb Rimecrest switchbacks, fight ice archers and Rimebound Golems, recover the summit bell and return. Golems warn their heavy slowing slam before impact.
- **Wake the Skyworks** — complete the mill quest, then speak to Fenna in Willowrest. Recover pump parts from ruined workshops, restore three sluices and defeat the reservoir guardians. Completion starts the water channels and turns the workshop gears.

Cliffs and reservoir water constrain the routes. Signposts and the expanded minimap guide exploration. New objectives, encounters, warnings and completion synchronize in co-op; host progress saves. Existing solo and party saves retain their original quests and receive the two new quests automatically. Quest rewards pay once.

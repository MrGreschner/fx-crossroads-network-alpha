# Business teaching workflow — V198.51

In Manage Class Pro, open **Venture access & progress** in the left sidebar.

1. Filter a class or module. Class spreadsheet shows student totals and one row per owned business. Read the last-save time before treating missing data as no activity. Scroll the spreadsheet horizontally for additional columns or export CSV.
2. Open Venture access. Select the class's students (or an individual), tick the premises you want to allow and save. Mixed boxes preserve individual differences until you change them. Students still have to acquire premises and fund expansion; permissions do not award cash or start a business.
3. Use Learning Academy to assign modules. The Modules sidebar tab lists module assignments. Assign one module at a time, or use the existing two-slot teacher policy where appropriate.
4. Students use Run & review to enter their classroom district. They establish a baseline, implement a decision, run further openings and revise from results. Record checkpoints at the indicated workdays, then explain the main outcomes using those records.
5. Review frozen submissions and the included gameplay checkpoints in the existing marking interface. Record completion after judging all main outcomes. An unsuccessful business can still demonstrate learning; a profitable one does not automatically earn credit.

## Typical operating sequence

| Level | Baseline | Test | Revised review |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Introductory | 3 full workdays | 7 total | 12 total |
| Intermediate | 4 | 10 | 18 |
| Advanced | 6 | 16 | 30 |

These are new active workdays in one focal venture after the module investigation starts. The baseline checkpoint fixes that venture. A workday lasts three active minutes; partial openings are excluded. These operating targets are part of a larger investigation, including research and discussion, not a timer that proves an outcome. Teachers can use the documented accommodation/equivalent-evidence field during assessment when the normal checkpoint route is inappropriate.

For example, ENT2030 students observe baseline demand, set an actual campaign budget and coordinate product/price/capacity, then use the resulting queues, lost sales, costs and profit to revise and retest. They do not submit an unchanged generic marketing-board screenshot as the whole practical task.

## Reading the statistics

- **Current workday** is the next/current opening number; **full days** counts completed full openings. Day 13 may mean 12 completed days.
- **Profitable days** counts openings with positive operating profit. Review costs, spoilage, service and debt alongside it.
- **Venture-days** in the student summary add across owned businesses. They do not mean calendar days or the age of one business. The per-venture table supplies that detail.
- **Current cash** is the sum of current business balances; district reserve is separate. Profit and cash differ because of stock, equipment, transfers and financing.
- **Approved restarts** comes from the retained teacher approval history. Current-run counters may start over while restart history remains visible.
- **No sync yet** means there is no available classroom save, not a mark of zero. Public-market progress is separate.

## Keep older work intact

New assignments use the new project edition. Existing graded packets retain their original work and assessment definition. Older active attempts can begin the new investigation from Run & review, but are not retroactively forced through a different completion contract. To require the new route for a fresh class, assign the current module edition.

Checkpoints are tied to the student's account and server save revision. The operating results are still reported by the student device; teacher judgement, discussion and observed demonstrations remain part of assessment. Only the main numbered outcomes are graded; supporting curriculum examples are teaching guidance.

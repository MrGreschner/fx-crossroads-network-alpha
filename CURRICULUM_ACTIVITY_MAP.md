A common hero or shop can be a shared context, but the assessed product, question and evidence should differ. Students should attach new module-specific work and explain what they contributed; copying the same reflection into several modules does not demonstrate new learning.

Copyright, work routines, competencies and pathways appear in several CTS courses by design. Relate them to the current project. Teachers may reuse a verified observation where appropriate, but the game never treats a journal checkbox as independent proof of behaviour. Digital simulations cannot establish physical tool safety or actual legal compliance.

## Business model assumptions

- Opportunity Board: score = need×weight + resources×weight + feasibility×weight. Ratings are student assumptions to be supported by research. Ties require a justified decision.
- Venture Pilot: stock purchases consume cash even when unsold; operating profit expenses only the stock sold. Reserve restricts affordable purchases. A supplier disruption increases unit cost by 35%; a demand-drop scenario sells into 60% of planned demand. Break-even is unavailable when price does not exceed unit cost. Explain limitations rather than claiming the model predicts a real venture.
- Merchandising A/B: visitors, stock, base unit cost and advertising budget are held constant. Only the selected price/packaging/service/place/promotion choice changes. Higher non-price choices have explicit added unit cost. Conversion uses a simplified reference price of 12; reach/conversion changes are deterministic. Stock-limited trials are flagged. Research real customer and merchandising evidence beyond this model.

## Teacher example use

Fail/pass/high-grade samples illustrate evidence quality, not a universal percentage rubric. The samples intentionally contain errors and missing work at the failing level. Unsubmitted modules remain ungraded, even where a concept or consistency check passes. Use the samples for moderation and discussion; never add them to a real student's assessment record.

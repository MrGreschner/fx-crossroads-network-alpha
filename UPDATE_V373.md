# Crossroads V373 — Complete hero unlock rollout

All 80 official heroes now have a defined availability route: 9 starters, 25 realm
champions, 7 activity goals and 39 remaining journey/co-op heroes. All 6 event
heroes also have earning routes. Custom and approved community heroes retain
their existing availability and are not assigned official-hero quests.

The nine starters remain Young Ram, Dribble, Living Book, Crossroads Cat,
Frostguard, Diamond Scout, Fairway Ranger, Crosswind Striker and Emberwright.
V371 realm champion routes and V372 achievement goals are retained.

## Hero Journeys

Challenge Hall / Challenge Lodge → Hero Journeys. Character Hall → select a locked
hero → View journey opens that hero's course card. The general invitation appears
after 3 different legitimate realm clears; prior qualifying clears count. These
are deliberately started instances, not scheduled NPC encounters. Listed locations
are the course settings, not newly assigned hero birthplaces. Nullweaver additionally
requires Astral Rift access. Event courses require their event to be active but do
not require three realm clears. Current winter 4 active / Halloween 2 inactive flags
are preserved; Halloween courses return when that event is enabled.

Courses use the existing production sprites, coloured contours, neutral front-facing
walking, combat, cooldowns and mobile movement. Stages include real interactions,
held repairs, ordered recipes/glyphs, a reflected beam puzzle, polarity coils, target
hits, charged-volley collisions, timed guards, warning dodges, physical shrinking gaps,
rewind return, gust routes, escorted followers/carts and combat defence. The instance
provides a temporary level-10 base kit where that hero's ability is required. Other
courses use the player's owned hero. Journey loaners cannot enter normal play.

Mara Dawnblade, Rune Duelist and Anjali require a final actual 1v1 after their courses.
Preparation persists; losing the duel does not erase the course. Free retries and
rematches grant no duplicate ownership rewards. Patch unlocks automatically after
3 completed human co-op realm adventures through the existing co-op result counter.

## Save safety and enforcement

Ownership schema v3 imports earlier v1/v2 claims and retains previously played
heroes with positive recorded damage or clears. Zero-use browsing records do not
unlock heroes. No account wipe is required. New journey preparation is bounded,
validated, included in portable profile data and practice rollback. Ownership checks
cover all 77 nonstarter official/event heroes in solo selection, network joining,
hero changes and random Arena picks. This keeps the existing trusted portable-profile
model; it is not a cryptographically signed anti-cheat ledger.

Journeys do not grant normal combat damage records, mastery points, CC/XP kill rewards,
realm clears, seed-cache rewards, farming progress or deathless streak wins/losses.
The course consumes no packed supplies. Exiting loads the normal town scene and
returns the owned hero; the temporary course kit cannot remain in normal town play.

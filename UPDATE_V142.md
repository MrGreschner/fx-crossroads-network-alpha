# v142 — Teacher examples, distinct business activities and rolling lobby crowns

## Teacher examples

In Teacher Mode, open **Class view → Example**. The Teacher assignment-review tab also has an **Example** button. Game Dev also has Class view and Example access. Choose **1 • Fail**, **2 • Pass** or **3 • High grade**.

Each fictional student has:
- Submitted **DES1020 Design Process** and **COM1145 Animation 1** work.
- Partly finished **CSE1110 Structured Programming 1** (40%) and **MAM1010 Marketing & Management** (50%) work.
- Journal questions and sample answers, original pixel artwork, playable prototype, ability frames, concept answers, code and a business-trial draft.
- Suggested outcome ratings, scores and feedback for the submitted modules.

Click a module to inspect evidence; **Mark outcomes** lets teachers practise changing ratings. Changes are kept only in that example session. Examples never enter saved classes or student profiles, never count as a real submission, and cannot send feedback to a real student. Downloaded example portfolios are named as examples. The numeric scores illustrate different quality levels; they are not official CTS grade boundaries. Routine and safety claims still require teacher observation in real assessment.

Examples can be shown through a classroom broadcast. Returning to real student records restores the private-assessment broadcast hold.

## Three new business challenges

Available to active Student Mode, Teacher Mode and Game Dev through the Education Guide; also through Market → Business classroom. Student assignment briefs link directly to the matching activity.

| Module | New activity | Distinct evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ENT1010 Challenge & Opportunity | Opportunity Board | Three needs/ideas, a weighted decision matrix, researched ratings, feasibility recommendation |
| ENT1020 Elements of a Venture Plan | Venture Pilot | Mission/resource/risk plan, normal and adverse scenarios, stock, cash reserve, break-even, critique |
| MAM1010 Marketing & Management | Merchandising A/B Lab | Same product/audience/stock/budget, one changed factor, units/revenue/costs/profit, limitations and recommendation |

These are independent teaching sandboxes: no real mine goods or market cash are spent. Results can be attached as frozen evidence and included in a player's normal profile download. Student Mode is required to record assessed work. Teacher/Game Dev can practise, but testing does not create assessed evidence.

Imported business reports must reproduce their results from their saved decisions. Automatic checks verify consistency, not research quality or outcome achievement. Numbers are simplified teaching models and cannot replace actual research, legal research or teacher assessment.

The existing idle Mine and timed Market remain available; these activities expand their educational use rather than replace their economies.

## Eight rolling lobby crowns

Public and code lobbies display a faint animated outline and crown for each eligible player. Crowns are cosmetic; no attack, health, speed or defence changes.

| Level | Crown | Damage during trailing 7 days |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | Bronze Spark | 1,000 |
| 2 | Copper Crest | 3,000 |
| 3 | Silver Scout | 7,000 |
| 4 | Golden Vanguard | 15,000 |
| 5 | Amethyst Regent | 25,000 |
| 6 | Sapphire Monarch | 40,000 |
| 7 | Ruby Champion | 65,000 |
| 8 | Celestial Crown | 100,000 |

Damage ages out after 168 hours, using one-minute buckets (expiry precision up to one minute). Monday's damage expires the following Monday. There is no Sunday reset. The profile shows damage, the current crown and the next threshold; hovering a lobby hero shows crown information. Guests update their cosmetics while waiting, including custom-hero players, without resending artwork or resetting health.

Crowns start tracking new eligible damage in v142; older calendar-week totals are not backfilled because they lack timestamps. History is saved in the portable player profile. Game Dev, Playtest, training targets and arena bot-practice damage do not count. Existing daily rewards and three calendar-week cosmetic rewards remain separate. Counts use the player's local device clock/profile; this is a cosmetic activity display, not a verified online leaderboard.

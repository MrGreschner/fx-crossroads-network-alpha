# Crossroads: Realms Beyond — V248

Complete artwork update based on V247. No deployment performed.

Neon Crucible's six enemies now have matte pixel artwork: Hatch Sentinel, Sweep Drone, Magnet Hauler, Repair Wisp, Fork Runner and Relay Weaver. The four Continuum shells—Forge, Archive, Reactor and Crown—retain their separate designs and substantial original presentation sizes. Hatch Sentinel's emergence reveals the new sprite instead of its old placeholder body. Existing skill/melee events now trigger the already established visual attack marker; timings, hazards and damage remain unchanged.

Chalkling retains its small cream chalk-block identity with clearer material shading and four poses. Eight remaining summon designs are refreshed: Mosaicling, Hive Drone, Stagelet (stage/mastery variant), Deputyling, Quillling, Lanternling, Gravehorse and Morrow Healer. Ordinary Merrymask puppets, mini basketball and Bit Buddy retain their previously accepted artwork. The original summon family selection, merging/weight scaling, tint, status rings, life spans and combat statistics remain.

All nineteen identities have idle, walk A, walk B and contained attack frames. The shared rendering adapter preserves original visible idle heights and foot anchors, with the existing individual actor draw rectangles and collision radii retained. Art routes cover solo and multiplayer enemy rendering, summons/revived creatures, Morrow's healer SVG alias, catalog portraits and the main-menu enemy gallery. Student-created artwork and Studio default templates are unchanged.

All gameplay content, enemy/hero definitions, boss progression, region geometry, economy, saves, town navigation and server authority remain V247. Original character and scenery art is retained. Only rendering adapters, visual timers, export inclusion and build stamps change.

# v149 — Crossroads Coins, starter market and town polish

Combat money is now Crossroads Coins (CC). New runs still start with 100. Bounties, item/food prices and coin rewards keep their existing balance. Save/network fields retain the internal `gold` key for compatibility. Coin Magnet is the renamed shop item.

Mine Gold, ore and gems stay in the mine wallet. Market cash stays in the business account. Basic setup buys five sorted ore units for 30 market cash, without needing a commissioned mine, and sets a simple strategy. Repeating it while stocked does not buy another pack. The first page explains stock, optional planning, business-day duration, opening and reports. Trading still takes 5 minutes–24 hours and continues while away. Larger product unlocks still need deeper mining.

Town projects keep their adventure/community requirements and add small material costs. Flowers, first bakery and the automatic wave-30 gate upgrade remain free. Costs are visible beside requirements. Mine Gold/ore/gems are deducted once when Build is pressed. Existing completed buildings are retained; no retroactive charges.

Gleam Atelier fills the unused path above Squirrel Post: visit to choose earned cosmetics, daily/weekly effects, titles and journey effects. Its doorway is clear and the building has collision.

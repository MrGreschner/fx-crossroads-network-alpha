# v131 — Arcane presentation, town expansion and Field Research

## Game Dev appearance
The previous effect has been replaced with an animated arcane architect insignia: a hovering crystal crest, drifting crystal fragments, moving ground sigil and orbiting sparks. Thin screen-corner accents replace the thick orange frame. The existing Game Dev powers and Classroom Broadcast remain available. Enter `gameDev`, then `R4ms`, through Elder Ram as before.

## Western town district
The town extends 800 world units farther west. Mountains, Ram Descent and the future mine sit on the new western edge. Crossroads Studio has its own clear plot west of Squirrel Post, and Relay Workshop has a separate southern plot with a side approach to its front door. Trees no longer overlap the Studio. The centre of town and eastern river remain in their existing coordinates. Space is reserved for future buildings.

The top Menu button now uses the same height, font and padding as Adventures.

## Mistglass Rift
The grid has been replaced by an oval stone sanctuary, animated mist, runic markings and portal lights. The practice arena fits the available viewport; enemies, summons and the player stay below the HUD. An obvious End Battle button confirms before returning to town, with replay still available. Rift battles remain practice without profile rewards.

## Daily challenge quick play
Click today's challenge on the main menu, in Adventures, or on your Profile card. The Profile challenge title and Play Today's Challenge button both work. Solo realm challenges start the selected realm directly; featured-hero challenges select that hero. Co-op and survival challenges open the appropriate multiplayer activity, where you still choose Solo or arrange your party and start normally. If already in a battle, you first confirm returning to town; that ends the attempt and leaves a live room.

Fifty shuffled tasks remain, now using early realms and survival targets of 5–10 floors/waves. These are designed as short daily attempts, roughly 20 minutes for a familiar player; completion time depends on skill and teammates. Rewards rotate deterministically by date through eight new animated cosmetics:

1. Solar Compass
2. Moonpetal Halo
3. Ember Crown
4. Tidal Pearls
5. Runic Orbit
6. Firefly Lanterns
7. Crystal Bloom
8. Aurora Ribbons

Rewards are cosmetic, expire at local midnight and travel with the downloaded profile. No combat statistics change. This is an offline date rotation, not a server-verified event system.

## Field Research — student playtesting mode
Anyone can type `playtest` to Elder Ram. No password is required: it grants observation tools, not combat powers or unlocks. Press F8 or use the Field Research button to show/hide its panel.

- Watch FPS and the recent low frame rate.
- Record up to 30 checkpoints with mode, damage and kills.
- Copy session evidence.
- Open the playtest notebook with evidence inserted into the editable method section; complete your own observations and export the report.
- Exit Research to restore the normal saved profile.

Combat uses your normal hero and build. Player progress, profile transfers and profile resets are disabled while researching. Existing progress remains safe; the mode ends on refresh or Exit Research. Test notes are separate from player progress. Switching into Game Dev ends the Research overlay, keeping the two modes distinct. Exiting while in a room leaves that game.

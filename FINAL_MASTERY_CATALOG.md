# Final mastery ability catalogue — 80 heroes

Choose alternatives for their play style; each includes a trade-off. Both can be equipped because they change different ability buttons. First techniques require 3 clears, 2 different realms and 10,000 damage. Tide Keeper and Spark Keeper instead need their Confluence Foundry co-op duet clear plus 10,000 damage on that hero. Final techniques require 12 clears, 6 different realms and 85,000 damage. Titles and critical milestones retain their own requirements.
